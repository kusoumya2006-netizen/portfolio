# SOUMYA KUMAR — PERSONAL & PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY AUDIT
*Project Soumya: Discovering the Person Before Designing the System*

## 1. Executive Discovery Findings

### Question 1: What makes Soumya's work distinctive?
Soumya Kumar does not treat software engineering and visual design as adjacent or conflicting hobbies; she operates at their exact intersection. Where most engineers consider UI an afterthought or default to unstyled component libraries, Soumya applies typographic rigor, color friction, and packaging-level spatial pacing. Conversely, where traditional graphic designers often create static mockups without understanding backend constraints, Soumya constructs the actual client state machines (CONVERA), API microservices (FastAPI in VYRA), multi-agent architectures (NEXORA), and cloud telemetry pipelines (MongoDB Atlas in NetWatch).

### Question 2: Which projects demonstrate the strongest abilities?
- **Technical Rigor**: **CONVERA** (Smart India Hackathon 2026, Problem SIH26264) demonstrates deterministic client-side triage algorithms, semantic overlap recognition, and an automated 95-test Vitest test suite.
- **Visual & Brand Direction**: **OFFSCRIPT** demonstrates mastery over brand personality, rebellious condensed typography, color discordance (hot-pink against warm ivory), and original character design.
- **Architectural Coordination**: **NEXORA** demonstrates complex multi-agent system thinking, coordinating acoustic feature extraction with contextual conversational risk analysis.

### Question 3: Which projects have the most interesting problem-solving stories?
- **CONVERA**: The realization that institutional grievance backlogs are rarely caused by a lack of administrators, but by redundant submissions across departmental silos and subjective prioritization. The problem-solving story is about using semantic clustering and mathematical urgency scoring to replace administrative paralysis.
- **ResuMatch AI**: Rejecting opaque neural "black-box" scoring in career tools. Instead of giving candidates an unexplained percentage, Soumya engineered a transparent TF-IDF keyword extraction formula that shows candidates the exact missing skills and structural formatting flaws.
- **VÉLORA**: Rejecting maximalist skincare marketing clutter. The design narrative (*"Skin, without the noise. Less routine. More intention."*) emerged by stripping away extraneous packaging claims and focusing entirely on high-contrast studio lighting, minimalist typography, and functional bottle ergonomics.

### Question 4: Which projects demonstrate technical depth?
- **CONVERA**: Strict state machine architecture preventing invalid ticket status transitions, coupled with append-only PostgreSQL audit logging and 95 unit/integration tests.
- **NEXORA**: Decomposition of voice-cloning detection into isolated coordinator agents (Lead, Code, Docs, QA) with contextual coercion analysis.
- **VYRA**: End-to-end FastAPI microservice architecture with explainable linguistic threat feature maps.
- **NetWatch**: Background health probe daemon handling HTTP/ping latency measurements with timeseries persistence.

### Question 5: Which projects demonstrate visual judgment?
- **OFFSCRIPT**: Unapologetic editorial pacing, oversized condensed display gothic typography, angled framing slashes, and an expressive hand-drawn orangutan mascot. Published on Behance.
- **VÉLORA**: Extreme restraint, monochrome studio bottle art direction, faceted geometric 'V' logo accent, and clean cosmetic packaging hierarchy. Published on Behance.
- **QCIC Official Insignia**: Symbolic distillation of quantum superposition and orbital mechanics into a monochrome silver vector wave that serves as the official club identity.

### Question 6: What is missing from the professional narrative?
Previously, the portfolio presented projects as a disjointed list without explaining *how* Soumya's mind operates across both domains. The narrative needed to explicitly articulate:
- The transition from learning foundational computer science at SVCE to tackling real-world problem statements (SIH 2026).
- The philosophy that engineering provides structural durability while visual design provides human resonance.
- Concrete leadership evidence as Design Lead at QCIC and core design team member at CyberNova.

### Question 7: Which claims lack evidence?
- Vague claims about "serving thousands of users" or "enterprise deployments" were ungrounded.
- *Resolution*: Strictly classify all hackathon systems as evaluated `PROTOTYPE` builds, and conceptual brand identities (VÉLORA) as self-initiated studio projects.

### Question 8: Which content should be removed?
- Generic lifestyle hobbies (e.g. music listening habits, casual photography) that dilute the technical and design focus.
- Unnecessary audio player widgets or decorative waveform controls that pretend to be functional software.
- *Resolution*: Completed in V2/V3 by refocusing the studio practice entirely on Brand Building, Creative Technology, UI/UX Architecture, and Technical Writing.

### Question 9: Which sections are repetitive?
- Multiple overlapping project lists.
- *Resolution*: Establish a single unified **Selected Works** exhibition for the top 5 flagship projects, supported by a comprehensive, interactive **Project Atlas** that categorizes work into four distinct disciplines (Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Design & Brand Identity, Creative Technology).

### Question 10: Which work deserves deeper presentation?
- **CONVERA**: Detailed data flow, urgency scoring heuristics, and test suite verification.
- **NEXORA**: Agent coordination matrix and acoustic/contextual risk formula.
- **OFFSCRIPT & VÉLORA**: Complete typography specimen sheets, color swatches with hex codes, and visual language rules.
