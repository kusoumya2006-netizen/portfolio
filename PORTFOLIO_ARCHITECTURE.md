## 3. Visual Design System

- **Palette**: Deep Pine (`#0E1613`), Champagne Ivory (`#F4EDE4`), Muted Sage (`#9BB0A5`), Brushed Metallic Silver (`#B5B5AD`).
- **Surfaces**: Intentional contrast between dark atmospheric containers and high-legibility ivory typography.
- **Typography Tokens**:
  - `font-serif`: DM Serif Display for masthead declarations.
  - `font-sans`: Inter for high-density reading and layout grids.
  - `font-mono`: Monospace stamps for coordinates, SLA times, and architectural telemetry.
- **Anti-Patterns Prohibited**: No generic templates, no repetitive rounded cards, no excessive particle canvases, no unrelated lifestyle/hobby pillars.
